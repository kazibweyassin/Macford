BIOGRAPHY

Namukwaya Teddy Theresa is a Legal Associate at McFord Advocates. She is an Advocate of the High Court of Uganda with experience in commercial transactions, civil and criminal litigation, corporate advisory, and regulatory compliance. Her practice involves advising individuals, businesses, and organizations on a broad range of legal matters, including contract drafting and review, company law, employment law, land transactions, and dispute resolution.

She is also experienced in legal research, due diligence, regulatory compliance, corporate governance, and the preparation of legal opinions and transactional documents.

Teddy holds a Bachelor of Laws (LLB) degree and a Postgraduate Diploma in Legal Practice from the Law Development Centre, Uganda. She is an Advocate of the High Court of Uganda and a member of the Uganda Law Society and the East Africa Law Society.
