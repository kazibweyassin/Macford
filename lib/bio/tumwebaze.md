# Ampaire Tumwebaze

**Senior / Founding Partner · Corporate & Commercial Law**

Ampaire Tumwebaze, Esq. is a distinguished Corporate and Commercial Lawyer and Senior/Founding Partner at McFord Advocates, with broad experience spanning over 16 years advising on International Investment, Corporate Transactions, Banking And Finance, Project Finance, Infrastructure, Mergers And Acquisitions, Corporate Governance And Strategic Business Advisory.

He is recognised for combining technical legal expertise with Commercial and Strategic Insight, particularly in Complex Transactions, Institutional Advisory and Business Structuring. His experience spans High-Level Litigation, Corporate Advisory and Cross-Sector Transactions, including participation in the legal teams that successfully represented the President of the Republic of Uganda in the 2016 and 2021 Presidential Election Petitions.

Mr. Tumwebaze holds an LL.B (Hons) from Makerere University Kampala, a Postgraduate Diploma in Legal Practice (Post Grad Dip LP) from the Law Development Centre (LDC) Kampala, and an LLM in Corporate and Real Estate Finance from the University of Abidjan. He is a Commissioner for Oaths and brings a strong regional and international perspective to his practice.

He is a Proactive member of the Uganda Law Society, and the East African Law Society. He is an Active Alumni of the Uganda Law Development Centre (LDC), and an Active Alumni of the Makerere Law Society (MLS).

- Corporate Governance & Strategic/Commercial Advisory
- Banking, Finance & Securities
- Project Finance & Infrastructure
